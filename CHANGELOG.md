# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first version of the Jace Games desktop app.

- Jace Games in its own window: games, multiplayer, Live, Studio, your profile and Locker
- Sign in with Jace once; you stay signed in
- Menus and shortcuts: Back, Forward, Home, Games (Ctrl/Cmd+1), Live (2), Join a live game (3), Studio (4), Leaderboards (5), zoom and fullscreen
- Links to other sites open in your normal browser
- A friendly offline screen that reconnects by itself
- Remembers your window size and position
- Updates: one click on Windows and Linux (AppImage); a "new version" message with a download link on Mac and the .deb
